Privacy at Versa Player

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how Versa Player accesses, processes and, where applicable, transmits data when you use the Android application.

1. Operator and contact

Versa Player is an independent software project operated from Chile. Public project identifier: Versa Player / Marco-GoD. For general support that does not require sharing sensitive personal information, users may use the public GitHub Issues channel. A dedicated support and privacy email address will be published here before the Google Play production release for private requests.

2. Local-first approach

Versa Player is designed primarily as a local media player. Your media library, favorites, playlists, listening history, statistics, appearance preferences, equalizer, balance, Iris settings, and manually added lyrics or artwork are primarily processed on your device.

Versa Player does not provide its own user account and does not upload your music library to a Versa Player server.

3. Media access

Subject to Android permissions, Versa Player may read audio and, where applicable, video files to display and play your local media library, metadata and thumbnails. This access supports the app's core functionality.

4. Locally stored information

The app may store locally: favorites and playlists; hidden items and history; playback statistics and BPM; visual and playback preferences; manually imported or synchronized lyrics; manually selected artwork; and technical proof needed to remember a previously verified Premium entitlement.

The current configuration excludes app data from Android automatic backup. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove local information, except that a valid Premium purchase may be restored through Google Play.

5. Lyrics and artwork

Versa Player can display lyrics and artwork embedded in local files or manually added by the user. It can also open a web search at the user's explicit request. Versa Player does not automatically scrape web pages and does not enable a commercial remote lyrics or artwork provider by default.

Content pasted, imported or selected by the user is stored or associated locally. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the rights or permission needed for content they choose to add.

6. Advertising

The free edition may display native ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its partners may process data such as IP address, approximate location, advertising/device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics according to their policies and applicable consent choices.

Versa Player uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to provide consent and privacy choices. Once Premium entitlement is confirmed, Versa Player's normal flow does not request ads for Premium users.

7. Versa Premium and Google Play

Versa Premium purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Versa Player may receive technical purchase-state information required to grant, restore or revoke Premium access. Versa Player does not receive or store complete payment-card details.

8. Internet radio

The radio feature may query an external station directory and connect directly to the station selected by the user. Those services may receive ordinary network information, including an IP address. Some legacy radio streams may use HTTP instead of HTTPS.

9. Voice services

Voice features may rely on speech recognition or text-to-speech services provided by Android or by the provider configured on the device. Depending on that provider and user settings, voice or text may be processed on external servers. Versa Player does not control those providers' privacy practices.

10. No first-party analytics account system

Versa Player does not include its own analytics platform, Crashlytics, or Versa account system. External SDKs and services such as Google Mobile Ads, Google Play, or radio stations may still process information under their own policies.

11. User controls and retention

Versa Player provides local controls for history/statistics, appearance and app-owned caches. Android also lets users clear application data or uninstall the app. Local data is generally retained until the user removes it, resets the application or uninstalls it. Third-party retention is governed by the respective provider.

12. Security

Internal information is stored using Android private app storage where applicable, and Versa Player aims to minimize transmitted information. Google and configured services use their supported secure channels, while some radio streams depend on the station's own configuration.

13. Children

Versa Player is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically designed as a children's product. Final Google Play audience settings and any special child-related treatment will follow the actual distribution and applicable law.

14. Rights and requests

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or portability of personal data. Because most Versa Player data remains on your device, many controls can be exercised directly through the app or Android. For general questions, the public support channel above may be used; private privacy requests should be sent to the dedicated email address that will be published before the Google Play production release. Third-party data may also be subject to the relevant provider's request mechanisms.

15. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features, providers, legal requirements or practices change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

16. External services

See Third-party services for a summary of external services associated with Versa Player.
